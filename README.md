## How Cashback Sites Actually Work (The Part Nobody Explains)

Here's the unsexy mechanics. When you click from a cashback site to a retailer and buy something, the retailer pays a commission to the cashback site for sending you. That commission is the "cashback."

The difference between sites is what happens next:

- **Rakuten** keeps a slice of that commission and passes the rest to you.
- **TopCashback** passes 100% of the commission to you and makes its money from ads, sponsored placements, and volume bonuses merchants pay for sending lots of shoppers.
- **BeFrugal**, **RebatesMe**, **Mr. Rebates** and others sit somewhere in between, often competing on rate but with smaller store catalogs.

This is why, when you run the same purchase through a comparison tool like CashbackMonitor, you'll see TopCashback listing the highest rate at most major retailers — sometimes by a wide margin. It's not a marketing claim; it's the structural consequence of giving back the full commission.
